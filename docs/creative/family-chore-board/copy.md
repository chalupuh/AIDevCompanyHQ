## C1d Full description (iOS and Google Play, limit 4000 each)

```
One shared chore board for the whole household.

HiveBoard puts everyone's chores on a single board that lives on one shared phone or tablet. Add your household, set the chores once, and they repeat every week. Members tap a chore to stamp it done. That is the whole idea.

FOR EVERY KIND OF HOUSEHOLD
- Family with kids: big, friendly tap targets that work even before a child can read, plus optional points and rewards.
- Couples and roommates: split the dishes, laundry and trash, and see at a glance what is done.
- Farms, barns and properties: feed the horses, muck out stalls, fill the water troughs. Chores can be shared between you and a helper, and points can stay off for adults.

HOW IT WORKS
- Add up to 8 household members. Each is a Kid or an Adult.
- Create chores, pick the days they repeat, and assign them to one or more members.
- Members open their own chore view and tap to complete. Done chores get a check and a small celebration.
- Choose the day your week starts. Your board resets on schedule.

POINTS AND REWARDS, IF YOU WANT THEM
Points and rewards are a switch for each member. Turn them on for a kid who loves filling the honey jar and trading points for an ice cream outing. Leave them off for adults, who still get a clear "done for today" view. You can set points to reset weekly, monthly or quarterly, or never.

A MANAGER AREA FOR GROWN-UPS
Setup lives behind a 4-digit PIN that any adult in the household can use. Members use the board without a PIN. Purchases and links are never shown on the board.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No ads.
- No accounts.
- No tracking SDKs.
- Data stays on your device.
- Data not collected.
You can export your household as a file and import it on another device whenever you like.

PAY ONCE
Try everything free for 14 days. After that, HiveBoard keeps working in demo mode with 1 member and 3 chores, or unlock the full app with a one-time purchase of $4.99. No subscription. Your chores and members are never deleted when the trial ends.

MORE
- Sounds and gentle haptics for key moments, with a Sounds switch to mute them.
- Optional daily reminder, off by default.
- Large tap targets, readable contrast, and Reduce Motion support.
- Works on iPhone and iPad (App Store) and on Android phones and tablets (Google Play).
- English only for now.

One board. Everyone's chores. Pay once.
```
