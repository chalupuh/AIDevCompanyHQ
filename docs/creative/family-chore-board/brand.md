# HiveBoard brand brief

Slug: `family-chore-board` · App repo: `C:/Users/goldg/family-chore-board` · Owner: creative-director
Sources: `docs/specs/family-chore-board.md` (AC-52..57, AC-77, AC-78), `.ux.md` section 1 ("Sticker Hive"), `docs/design-tokens.md` (matches the shipped `src/ui/tokens.ts`).
Store position: **Lifestyle** category (owner, 2026-11-11), not Kids / Designed for Families. Audience: any household (family with kids, couple, roommates, farm or barn).

## 1. Personality

A well-made wooden toy on the kitchen table: warm, bouncy, tactile, generous, and calm underneath. Premium without being babyish. Kind and specific, never pushy.
- Board side: sunny, chunky, playful ("sticker book made of honeycomb").
- Manager side: quiet, clear, trustworthy ("the kitchen-table office").
- Promise in one line: *One shared chore board for the whole household. Pay once, no accounts, nothing leaves the device.*

## 3. Type

- **Fredoka** (SemiBold/Bold): headlines on screenshots, feature graphic wordmark, video titles.
- **Nunito** (Regular/Bold): sub-captions, small print.
- Both SIL OFL (bundled via @expo-google-fonts). All text is overlaid in post (Figma/ImageMagick/ffmpeg), **never generated by the image model**.
- Screenshot headline: Fredoka Bold, ink #2B1A0F, max 2 lines, max ~28 characters per line, at least 90 px cap height on a 1290-wide canvas.

## 4. Illustration style (prompt language)

Base style block (paste into every image prompt):
> flat claymation-inspired illustration, chunky rounded shapes, thick warm dark-brown outline (#2B1A0F), hard offset drop shadow straight down, die-cut sticker look, soft paper grain texture, warm cream background (#FFF7E6), limited palette of honey yellow (#FFC93C), meadow green (#1F7A4D), plum (#4A2C8F) and pastel accents, rounded hexagon shapes, friendly and calm, simple composition, centered subject, generous empty space, no text, no letters, no logos

Negative prompt (every job):
> text, letters, words, watermark, signature, logo, photo, photorealistic, realistic human, face, child, person, hands, fingers, gradient mesh, neon, glitter, 3d render plastic, busy background, clutter, blurry, jpeg artifacts, extra limbs, deformed

Motifs:
- **Honey jar**: squat rounded jar with a hex-shaped label area (blank), honey drops rising into it. The hero object.
- **Bee**: one original round bee, two oval wings, no face details beyond two dot eyes and a small smile, ink outline. Not a cartoon character with a name, not resembling any existing bee mascot. Used small, as a helper, never as the icon centrepiece next to the jar unless it reads at 48 px.
- **Rounded hexagons**: avatar tiles, check stamps, pattern at 4% opacity.
- **Household objects** for chores: bed, plant, bowl, toothbrush, broom, laundry basket, hay bale, water bucket, horseshoe, watering can. Objects, never people.
- **People**: represented only by the in-app hex avatars (initial or emoji on a tinted hex). No illustrated or photographed humans, especially no children, no hands.

## 5. Store screenshots: composition

- Background: paper #FFF7E6 with the 4% hex pattern; one honey blob shape behind the device.
- Device: real app screen captured from the simulator/emulator (Board look), placed in a plain rounded device frame (generic, no Apple/Google hardware logos), slight tilt max 4 degrees or none.
- Headline overlaid at the top (see section 3). One idea per screenshot.
- Demo data uses invented first names (Ava, Sam, Mia, Leo, Dana, Chris) and emoji/initial avatars only. At least one **Adult** member and one barn/farm chore set (AC-78). No prices or paywall visible on board screenshots (AC-71, AC-78).

## 6. Voice and words

Use: household, board, chores, members, done, honey jar, rewards, pay once, no accounts, on your device, ad-free.
Claims we can back (AC-31, AC-32, AC-52, AC-55): "No ads", "No accounts", "No tracking SDKs", "Data stays on your device", "Data not collected" (matches the store privacy forms), "One-time purchase", "14-day free trial".

## 7. Words and claims to avoid

- "100% secure", "unhackable", "COPPA certified", "kidSAFE", "teacher approved", "#1", "best", "award-winning", "doctor/psychologist recommended": unbacked.
- "Works offline forever", "free" alone (it is a trial, then $4.99 or demo limits). Say "14-day free trial, then one-time $4.99".
- "Kids app", "for children" as the main label, "Designed for Families", "Kids category": we are Lifestyle; families are one audience among several.
- Baby talk, "Yay!!!", streak shaming, "don't lose your streak", countdowns, guilt.
- "Sync", "cloud", "share with your partner's phone": not supported.
- "Chore" + competitor names (Chorely, ChoreHero, OurHome, etc.), any reference to other apps.
- "Hive" puns that imply the Hive brand or beekeeping software.

## 8. Originality (hard rules)

No imitation of other brands' logos, mascots, characters or trade dress (no lookalikes of any known cartoon bee or honey brand). No living artists' names or "in the style of <studio>" in prompts. No real people, no children's faces, no photographic humans. No generated text inside images.
