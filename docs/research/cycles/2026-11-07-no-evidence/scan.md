## Longlist (12)

### 1. trip-packing-list: Reusable packing lists by trip type (simple utility)
- **Idea:** Packing lists built from trip templates (weekend, beach, business, camping) with per-person lists and a "packed" mode. Works fully offline.
- **Category / platforms:** Travel; iOS + Android (mobile-first).
- **Demand signals:** *assumption*: packing apps are a long-standing travel sub-category, and travel peaks around the holidays and in early-year planning. No link.
- **Pain signals:** *assumption*: free packing apps lean on ads, accounts and paywalled templates.
- **Competing apps:** Existing packing-list apps in the stores. *assumption*: names, ratings and review counts not verified.
- **The gap:** No account, templates free, one-time unlock for extras, and a "last trip" copy-forward.
- **Ease:** ~5. **Quick take: Warm** (unverified).

### 2. holiday-gift-budget: Gift list and budget tracker (simple utility, seasonal)
- **Idea:** Who, what, budget, bought/wrapped status, one running total, reusable each year.
- **Category / platforms:** Lifestyle / Finance-light; iOS + Android + web.
- **Demand signals:** *assumption*: strong seasonal peak from Nov to Dec every year; the brief itself lists holiday planning as a timing target.
- **Pain signals:** *assumption*: generic notes and spreadsheets are used; existing apps are ad-heavy or seasonal clutter.
- **Competing apps:** *assumption*: several gift-list apps exist; none verified.
- **The gap:** Year-over-year history ("what did I give Mum last year"), privacy (a surprise list stays on the device), and no shared accounts.
- **Ease:** ~5. Timing risk: it must ship fast to catch this December. **Quick take: Warm**.
