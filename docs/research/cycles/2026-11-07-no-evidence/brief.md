**Theme:** Open scan
**Purpose:** Broad sweep for the next build — look beyond cycle 1's health/habits/productivity focus and surface genuinely new opportunities across any proven category.

## Targets
- Longlist of **~12 ideas** across **≥ 4 categories**, with overlaps merged

## Questions for the researcher
1. **Beyond productivity/habits:** What are the top-charging or fast-growing categories on the App Store / Play right now (travel, photography, home & garden, pets, food/diet, parenting, personal finance light) where a small team can build a simple, privacy-first alternative?
2. **Review-mining gaps:** Look at 1–3 star reviews of top apps in these new categories. What complaints keep appearing? ("too many ads", "needs accounts", "too bloated", "no offline mode", "no Android version")?
3. **Community demand:** Are there Reddit threads or Product Hunt launches from the last 3–6 months asking for something that no one has built simply yet — especially "I wish there was a lightweight version of X"?
4. **Seasonal/timing:** Anything peaking in late 2026 / early 2027 (holiday planning, New Year's resolution prep, tax season prep, spring cleaning) that we could ship a simple utility for?
