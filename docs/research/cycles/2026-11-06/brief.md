# R&D Cycle Brief: 2026-11-06 (Cycle 1)

**Theme:** Open scan (no theme)
**Purpose:** first cycle. Find a portfolio of ideas and, above all, at least one **fast win** that lets the whole studio pipeline be exercised end to end.

## Targets
- A longlist of **~20 ideas** across **at least 5 categories**, such as Health & Fitness, Productivity, Personal Finance, Lifestyle/Home, Education, Utilities, Small-business tools.
- At least **6 simple proven utilities** (Ease 4–5, e.g. trackers, timers, checklists, calculators) and **3–5 more ambitious** ideas.
- The analyst scores the top **~8** Hot/Warm entries.

## Constraints
- Platforms: web and/or mobile (React Native/Expo, Next.js). No hardware, no wearables-only apps.
- The first build should be an MVP of **≤ 4 weeks** (Ease ≥ 4). Ambitious ideas are allowed but should be marked as later bets.
- Originality gate (METHODOLOGY §3) applies. Flag regulated areas (medical, financial advice, children's data).

## Questions for the researcher
1. In top-charting simple-utility categories, what complaints recur in 1–3★ reviews (ads, paywalls, bloat, privacy, missing platform)?
2. Which popular general apps are used awkwardly by a specific niche (e.g. ADHD, shift workers, students, caregivers, freelancers)?
3. What "is there an app that…" requests come up repeatedly on Reddit, Product Hunt or forums?
4. Are there privacy-first / offline-first / no-account angles that reviewers ask for?
